Baboon Lake, Sierra Nevada, W USA
Cosmogenic data from Borchers et al. (2016), Marcott et al. (2019), and Moore and Granger (2019).
Erosion rate set to 0 mm/ka for all samples.
Sampling year for Borchers et al. (2016) and Moore and Granger (2019) samples based on sample names.
Calibration age based on Clark and Gillespie (1997) 14C age of 11190 ± 70 BP. Calibration using the
IntCal20 curve yields an age of 13102 ± 75 cal BP. Plus 57/58/65 years for sampling year.
Calibration age uncertainty of 250 years assumed.
Three 10Be production rates are excluded (SNV-02, SNV-03, SNV-08) to fulfill the clustering
criterion.
Isostatic adjustment calibration data: ICE7G.

Billingen Baltic ice lake drainage, S Sweden
Cosmogenic data from Stroeven et al. (2015).
Sample elevations updated based on LiDAR 1 m elevation. Single 26Al measurement from Stroeven et al.
(2016).
Calibration age based on correlation of clay varve record and the δ18O NGRIP record: drainage event
age = 11620 cal BP. Plus 53 years for sampling year. A combined uncertainty of 300 years has been
assigned to the clay varve/ice core correlation and emergence history. Calibration carried out using
the isostatic adjustment for shielding/production under water (isostsubm), with the PD2015 (Påsse
and Daniels 2015) uplift history modified by a factor of 1.13 (isostsubmmod) to match the highest
shoreline at 122-136 m a.s.l. (interpolated by the Swedish Geological Survey) at the sample
locations and the time of deglaciation.
Isostatic adjustment calibration data: PD2015 modified by 1.13 (isostPmod).

correct (based on email contact with Margaret Jackson).

curve of the IntCal20 and the SHCal20 calibration curves yields an age of 21293 ± 161 cal BP. Plus
62 years for sampling year. Uncertainty of 500 years assumed.
Isostatic adjustment calibration data: ICE7G.
